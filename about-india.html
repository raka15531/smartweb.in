Our Story

Founded in 2019 by Akash Singh, NextGen Web Solutions began as a one-person operation with a simple mission: to help Indian businesses establish a professional online presence at affordable prices.

Starting from a small office in Faridabad, we've grown to serve clients across 50+ cities in India, delivering 500+ successful website projects. Our journey has been fueled by our commitment to quality, transparent pricing, and exceptional customer support.

Today, we're proud to be one of the most trusted website development companies in India, known for our reliability, technical expertise, and dedication to client success.
